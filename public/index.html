About Us

Cinema with devotion,
stories with soul

Sri Peddammathalli Creations is a proprietorship film production company based in Chengicherla, Hyderabad. It is named after Sri Peddamma Thalli, the mother goddess revered across Telangana, and we begin every project with her blessings.

Under the leadership of Proprietor & Producer Sri Adepu Harish, the company develops, produces and presents Telugu feature films. We look for fresh writing, honest characters and production values that hold up on the big screen.

The company is enrolled with the Telugu Film Chamber of Commerce (TFCC), Hyderabad as a Provisional Life Member in the Producer Sector. This lets it represent productions before the Telangana & A.P. State Film, Television & Theatre Development Corporations and the Central Board of Film Certification.
